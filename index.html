Tanmay AIOS

A private, personal automation tool. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

Tanmay AIOS is a personal "AI operating system" used only by its owner, Tanmay Dani. It runs on the owner's own computer and works with the owner's own Google account (tanmayjdani@gmail.com) to:

  • Read and organize the owner's email (Gmail)
  • Create and update the owner's documents, spreadsheets and presentations (Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides)
  • Read and update the owner's calendar and to-do lists (Google Calendar, Google Tasks)

Typical uses: tracking business bookings and payouts, keeping personal finance records, and preparing daily summaries for the owner.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There is no sign-up, no other users, and no public service.
