Privacy Policy

Tanmay AIOS · Last updated 18 October 2026

Tanmay AIOS is a private automation tool used only by its owner, Tanmay Dani. This policy explains how the tool accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Who uses this app

Only the owner, with his own Google account. The app is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

Google data the app accesses

  • Gmail: read, label and send the owner's email.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides: read, create and edit the owner's files.
  • Google Calendar: read and edit the owner's events.
  • Google Tasks: read and edit the owner's to-do lists.
  • Basic profile: the owner's name and email address, to confirm which account is signed in.

How the data is used

Only to carry out tasks the owner asks for: organizing email, tracking bookings and finances, updating spreadsheets and documents, and preparing summaries. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models for anyone else.

How the data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on that computer. Any copies of data the app creates stay on that computer or in the owner's own Google account.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party. When the owner uses an AI assistant on his own computer to run tasks, the data is processed only for those tasks at his direction.

Limited Use

Tanmay AIOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading or changing any Google data. Local credentials can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
